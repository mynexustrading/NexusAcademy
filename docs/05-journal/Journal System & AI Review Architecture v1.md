# **6\. AI Journal Review Assistant (v1)**

Purpose:

Review behavior.

Not predict markets.

---

❌ Generate signals

❌ Give entries

❌ Predict direction

❌ Act as mentor

---

AI reviews:

---

## **Review A**

Completeness

Missing stop loss information.

---

## **Review B**

Risk Discipline

Example:

---

## **Review C**

Reflection Quality

Example:

---

## **Review D**

Pattern Recognition

Example:

---

## **Review E**

Behavior Trend

Example:

# **11\. Database Design Principles**

Never store:

Raw text only

---

Store:

### **Structured Fields**

AND

### **Reflection Text**

Example:

Pair \= XAUUSD

Direction \= Buy

Risk \= 1%

RR \= 2.5

Reflection \= Text

This allows:

* Analytics  
* AI Review  
* Statistics  
* Future Nexus OS
