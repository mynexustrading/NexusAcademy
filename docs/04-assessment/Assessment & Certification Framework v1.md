↓

Get Certificate

Learn  
↓

↓  
Transform  
↓  
Certify

---

# **2\. Assessment Philosophy**

Nexus Academy does NOT certify:

Knowledge

alone.

Nexus Academy certifies:

Knowledge  
\+  
Application  
\+  
Behaviour

---

# **3\. Assessment Architecture**

Every Stage has 3 assessment layers:

---

## **Layer A — Knowledge Assessment**

Purpose:

Validate understanding.

Tools:

* Lesson Quiz  
* Module Quiz  
* Stage Quiz

Requirement:

Minimum Pass Score: 80%

---

## **Layer B — Application Assessment**

Purpose:

Validate execution.

Tools:

* Journal Submission  
* Trade Plan Submission  
* Reflection Exercise

Requirement:

Evidence must be submitted.

---

## **Layer C — Behaviour Assessment**

Purpose:

Validate consistency.

Tools:

* Journal consistency  
* Risk compliance  
* Workflow adherence

Requirement:

Student demonstrates repeatable process.

---

# **4\. Stage Completion Formula**

Stage Completion requires:

Knowledge Pass

AND

Application Pass

AND

Behaviour Pass

Failure in one area:

Stage Incomplete

---
